Artificial intelligence is a field of computer science.
Machine learning is a method of learning patterns from data.
Neural networks contain layers of connected mathematical units.
Transformers use attention mechanisms to process sequences.
Language models learn to predict the next token in a sequence.
Training a neural network involves calculating loss and updating weights.
Large language models capture complex patterns from vast amounts of text.
Generative AI can create text, images, music, and code.
Reinforcement learning uses rewards and punishments to train agents.
Deep learning has enabled significant progress in computer vision and natural language processing.
Bias in training data can lead to unfair outcomes in AI systems.
Ethical AI development requires careful consideration of societal impact.
Future AI systems will likely integrate multiple modalities like vision, language, and action.
